Frequently Asked Questions

How long does the recruitment process take?
Typically, the process takes between 4 to 8 weeks. This includes initial application review, aptitude testing, medical examination, and the final interview.
Can I apply without prior experience?
Yes! Many of our entry-level positions require no prior experience. We provide full paid training to equip you with the necessary skills.
What happens after I submit my application?
You will receive an automated confirmation email. Our recruitment team will review your CV, and if you meet the initial criteria, we will contact you for an interview.
