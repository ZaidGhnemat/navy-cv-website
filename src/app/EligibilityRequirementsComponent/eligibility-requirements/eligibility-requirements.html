Eligibility Requirements

Age

Candidates must be between 18 and 35 years old at the time of application.

Education

Minimum High School Diploma. Technical roles require relevant certifications or degrees.

Physical

Must pass the standard Navy Physical Readiness Test (PRT) and medical examination.

Citizenship

Proof of citizenship or permanent residency is mandatory for security clearance.

Legal

No felony convictions. Background check and security screening required.

Documents

Valid ID, CV/Resume, and Educational transcripts ready for upload.
